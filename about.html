How we work

Four things we commit to on every engagement.

Transparency
You see all bids

We show you every competitive offer we receive — not just the ones that work best for us. You choose. We explain the tradeoffs.

Timing
Supplier-led quote timing

Submit your information today. You receive competing offers after suppliers return pricing, and we handle enrollment once you choose.

Independence
No supplier affiliations

We have no financial relationship with any specific supplier beyond standard broker compensation that's paid by the supplier, not you. The best rate always wins.

Long-term
Renewal monitoring

We set a reminder for your contract expiration and reach out 90 days early. You never auto-renew at holdover rates. Every renewal is re-bid.
