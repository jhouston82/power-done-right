Day-to-day

What the work actually looks like.

Deal flow
Active pipeline

New procurement engagements come in weekly. Each one involves collecting usage data, submitting to suppliers, managing the bid process, and presenting options to the client.

Market analysis
Daily market read

Futures settlement, ISO market data, storage reports, weather forecasts — the team tracks what's moving the market and translates it into actionable client guidance.

Client portfolio
Long-term relationships

Most clients have 12–36 month contracts that require renewal management. The portfolio grows over time as clients renew and refer new accounts — high intrinsic value per relationship.

Supplier network
90+ active relationships

We maintain active relationships with suppliers across all markets. Knowing which suppliers are hungry for new accounts — and which aren't — is a real competitive advantage.

What we offer

What you get working at PDR.

Competitive compensation

Sales roles have base + uncapped commission tied directly to client savings. Analyst and operations roles are salaried. Compensation reviewed annually.

Remote-first, no HQ requirement

The entire team operates remotely. No relocation, no required office presence. Work from wherever you're most productive.

Direct access to leadership

Small team means direct involvement in strategy, client relationships, and how the business is built. No layers of management between you and the decisions that matter.

Merit-based advancement

Titles and compensation track performance, not tenure. People who move the business forward get recognized quickly.
