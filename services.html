Read your bill like we do

Your bill has two sides. Only one is negotiable.

Every commercial bill combines charges set by your supplier and charges set by your utility. We put suppliers in competition for the first side, decide how capacity and pass-through charges are handled in the contract, and leave delivery with your utility.

  • Supply: we shop it

    Suppliers compete on the energy rate for your account.

  • Capacity and pass-throughs: we structure them

    Grid charges a supplier can either lock into your price or pass along to you as they come. It's a contract choice, and we show you which on every offer.

  • Delivery: stays with your utility

    Wires, meters and outage response don't change when you switch.

Contract structure

The rate is half the job. The contract is the other half.

Competition gets you a good number. How the contract is built decides what you actually pay over its life — so we structure it around your load, not around whatever the supplier sent first.

Your contract, decided line by lineIllustration

Product

Fixed is one price for the whole contract. Index follows the market month to month. Block-and-index locks in part of your usage and lets the rest float. The right choice depends on whether your business can handle a bill that moves — and we tell you plainly which one fits how you use power.

Term length

Most business contracts run one to three years. Longer is not automatically cheaper — it depends on where prices for future months sit the day you sign. We tell you which length makes sense in today's market.

Timing

We watch market prices for the months ahead to time the purchase, and we shop before your current contract ends — not after, when it rolls onto default or holdover pricing.

The fine print

Early termination fees, penalties if your usage moves outside a set range, and charges added on top of the rate. We line every offer up the same way so you are comparing like with like.

Across your sites

Operate several locations? Where it helps, we line up contract end dates so your portfolio can be shopped as one account instead of one meter at a time.
