Broker Disclosures

Compensation structure, licensing, supplier relationships, and regulatory notices for Power Done Right LLC — an independent commercial energy broker licensed through the BOX network.

Effective May 1, 2026  ·  Last updated May 1, 2026

1. Broker Compensation Disclosure

Important notice

Power Done Right LLC is compensated by the energy supplier selected by the client — not by the client directly. Our services are provided at no direct cost to your business. Supplier compensation is embedded in the contract rate as a per-unit adder and does not increase the price you pay above what the supplier would otherwise offer.

When PDR facilitates an energy contract on your behalf, the winning supplier pays PDR a brokerage fee. This fee is structured as a per-unit adder applied to the volume consumed under the contract. Typical adder ranges are:

  • Electricity: $0.001–$0.004 per kWh, depending on market, contract length, and account size
  • Natural gas: $0.01–$0.03 per therm (or MMBtu equivalent), depending on market and contract length

These ranges are representative and may vary. The specific adder applicable to your contract will be disclosed upon request before you sign any supplier agreement. You may request written confirmation of the exact compensation amount at any time by contacting us at disclosures@powerdoneright.com.

PDR does not charge clients origination fees, consulting fees, or any other direct fees. All compensation flows from suppliers.

2. Independence from Suppliers

PDR is an independent broker with no ownership, financial interest, or exclusive contractual obligation with any retail energy supplier. We maintain active relationships with more than 90 licensed retail electricity and natural gas suppliers across our markets. No supplier is given preferential treatment in the rate solicitation process. The supplier offering the most competitive terms for your specific load profile and contract requirements is the supplier we recommend.

PDR employees and principals do not hold equity positions in any retail energy supplier. Suppliers do not pay PDR retainers, annual fees, or any form of compensation other than the per-unit adder described above.

3. Supplier Relationships and Conflicts of Interest

Because PDR is compensated by suppliers, a general conflict of interest exists between PDR's economic interest in placing your account and your interest in obtaining the lowest possible total rate. PDR manages this conflict through the following practices:

  • Open-market solicitation: Every account is submitted to all qualified suppliers in the relevant market, not a preferred subset.
  • Rate transparency: We present all qualifying supplier responses to clients in a side-by-side format, including the supplier's all-in rate.
  • No supplier volume commitments: PDR has no quota or volume guarantee with any supplier that would incentivize steering accounts.
  • Client-directed selection: The final supplier selection is always made by the client, not by PDR.

4. No Utility Affiliation

Power Done Right LLC is not affiliated with, owned by, or financially connected to any regulated electric or gas utility, including but not limited to Oncor, CenterPoint Energy, ComEd, AEP, PPL Electric, PECO, Eversource, National Grid, or any other distribution utility. PDR operates exclusively as a third-party broker in the competitive retail energy market.

PDR does not represent any utility in any market, and utilities do not compensate PDR in any form.

5. Licensing and Registration

PDR serves customers through the BOX network, which holds the retail energy broker registrations and licenses under which PDR operates. Copies of the applicable registrations are available on request (see Section 8).

Electricity: Texas, California, Connecticut, Delaware, Illinois, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New York, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Rhode Island, Washington, D.C..

Natural gas: Arizona, Colorado, Florida, Indiana, Kentucky, Michigan, Virginia.

New York: New York State Department of Public Service — dps.ny.gov or 1-800-342-3377.

For regulatory contact information in other states, please contact us directly at disclosures@powerdoneright.com.

7. No Guarantee of Savings

PDR makes no guarantee that the rates obtained through our procurement process will be lower than your current rate or lower than any benchmark. Energy markets are volatile. Rates obtained through competitive bidding may be higher than prevailing index prices at any given moment depending on market conditions, contract length, and risk premium. PDR's role is to ensure your account receives the most competitive offers available from qualified suppliers on the day of solicitation — not to guarantee an absolute price level.
