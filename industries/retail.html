How it works

You choose the supplier. We make them compete.

01

Utility still delivers

Your local utility keeps maintaining the wires and delivering power to your location. Nothing changes on-site, and reliability is unaffected — only who supplies the energy.

02

You choose the generator

In a deregulated market, the energy supply portion of your bill is open to competition. You pick which company generates and sells the electricity — and at what rate.

03

We make suppliers compete

We submit your load profile to 90+ vetted suppliers at once. They bid against each other, the lowest bid wins your contract — and we charge no fees to your business.

Contract structures

The right structure depends on how you budget and your appetite for risk.

Fixed rate

Locked price per kWh

One price per kWh for the full term, regardless of what markets do — so the energy line in your budget stays predictable all year. Best for operators who want certainty and zero surprises.

Index / passthrough

Market rate, no markup

Your rate floats with the monthly ISO settlement price. You take market risk but capture the downside when wholesale rates fall. Best for businesses comfortable riding the market when conditions favor buyers.

Hybrid

Fixed base + float index

Lock part of your load at a fixed rate and leave the rest on index — balancing certainty against upside and letting you layer in pricing over time. Best for larger or multi-site operations that want certainty on most of the load.
