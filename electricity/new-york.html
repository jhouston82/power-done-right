New York energy choice

New York businesses can choose their electricity supplier.

New York is a deregulated electricity market, which means commercial customers can choose the company that supplies their energy. The grid is operated by the New York ISO (NYISO), and your local utility keeps delivering the power over its wires no matter who you buy supply from. The poles, meters, and outage response stay with that regulated utility — only the supply portion of your bill is open to competition, and that's the part we shop for you.

We work with businesses across New York's utility territories, including Con Edison, National Grid, NYSEG, RG&E, Central Hudson, and Orange & Rockland. If you're not sure which territory you're in, your meter and bill will tell us, and we'll confirm it for you.

Switching in New York

You change the rate. Nothing changes at your meter.

01

Your utility keeps delivering

Con Edison, National Grid, NYSEG still owns the wires, reads your meter, and restores power after outages. Switching suppliers does not touch any of that — there is no interruption to your service.

02

You choose the supplier

In a competitive market, you pick which retail provider sells you the energy — and at what rate and term. That's the line on your bill where the savings live.

03

We make suppliers compete

We put your load profile in front of 90+ vetted suppliers at once. We collect their best pricing, bring you the competing offers side by side, and handle the paperwork — at no fee to your business.
