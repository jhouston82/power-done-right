Privacy Policy

How Power Done Right collects, uses, and protects the information you share when requesting commercial energy rate quotes.

Effective May 1, 2026  ·  Last updated May 1, 2026

1. Who We Are

Power Done Right LLC ("PDR," "we," "us," or "our") is a licensed independent commercial energy broker operating in deregulated US markets. Our registered office is in Texas. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle personal and business information collected through powerdoneright.com and related services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when submitting a rate quote request or contacting us:

  • Business contact information — name, title, company name, business phone number, and business email address
  • Facility information — service address, utility account details, commodity type (electricity or natural gas), and estimated usage
  • Current contract information — contract expiration date and current rate, if provided
  • Communications — messages sent through our contact form or email

We do not collect payment card numbers, Social Security numbers, or other sensitive personal identifiers through this website.

We also collect limited technical data automatically when you visit our site, including IP address, browser type, pages visited, and referral source. This data is collected through Google Analytics and is used in aggregated, anonymized form.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Solicit competitive rate quotes from licensed energy suppliers on your behalf
  • Contact you with rate results, market updates, and renewal reminders
  • Comply with licensing and regulatory obligations in deregulated energy markets
  • Improve our website and services based on aggregated usage patterns

We do not use your information for automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

4. Sharing Your Information with Suppliers

To obtain competitive rate quotes, we share your facility and usage information with licensed retail energy suppliers participating in our bidding process. We share only the minimum information necessary — typically service address, utility account number, commodity, and usage profile — and do so under the understanding that suppliers will use this data solely to prepare and submit rate offers.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

We may disclose information when required by law, court order, or regulatory authority, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of PDR or others.

5. Cookies and Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser that collect information such as how long you spent on a page and which pages you visited. This data is processed by Google under their own privacy policy. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party retargeting technologies.

6. Data Retention

We retain business contact and facility information for up to three years from the date of last contact or contract expiration, whichever is later, to support contract renewal monitoring and regulatory recordkeeping. After this period, data is securely deleted or anonymized.

7. Data Security

We store form submission data in a secured cloud database with role-based access controls and encrypted connections. Access to customer records is restricted to PDR personnel with a legitimate business need. While no system is completely immune to breach, we follow industry-standard practices to protect your information.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your state of residence, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Opt out of future marketing communications

California residents have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what categories of personal information we collect and whether we sell personal information (we do not). Texas residents have rights under the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act (TDPSA).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@powerdoneright.com. We will respond within 45 days.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are directed exclusively to businesses and business professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18 years of age. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
