Section 08 · FAQ

Questions CFOs ask us first.

How do you get paid?

The supplier you choose pays us a per-kWh fee out of their margin — disclosed in your contract. You pay nothing out of pocket, and our recommendation is the same regardless of which supplier you pick. We're independent.

Are you a supplier or a broker?

A broker. We don't generate or sell electricity ourselves. We run reverse auctions across 90+ retail energy suppliers in the BOX network — including Constellation, NRG/Direct Energy, AEP Energy, Engie, Shell Energy, and TXU — and bring you the winning offer.

Will my service be interrupted when I switch?

No. Your local utility still delivers the power and reads your meter. We're swapping the supplier behind the meter, which happens at your next billing cycle with zero on-site change.

What if I'm mid-contract?

We'll still audit you. About 70% of the contracts we look at have a renewal window inside the next 12 months — we flag yours, lock in tomorrow's rate today (a "blend & extend"), and queue the switch for your contract end date.

Which states do you cover?

Every US deregulated market — including TX, PA, NJ, NY, OH, IL, MD, MA, CT, DC, DE, ME, NH, RI, VA, and CA — plus deregulated gas markets across the country. If your business has sites in multiple states, we consolidate them under one agreement.

How long does the process take?

You spend about 5 minutes submitting your info. First competitive bids arrive within 24 hours. When you pick a rate, you'll sign a Letter of Authorization and a one-page pricing contract — both take under 10 minutes. The switch happens on your next billing cycle, typically 2–4 weeks later.
