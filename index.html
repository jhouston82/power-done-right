Read your bill like we do

Your bill has two sides. Only one is negotiable.

Every commercial bill combines charges set by your supplier and charges set by your utility. We put suppliers in competition for the first side, decide how capacity and pass-through charges are handled in the contract, and leave delivery with your utility.

  • Supply: we shop it

    Suppliers compete on the energy rate for your account.

  • Capacity and pass-throughs: we structure them

    Whether these are fixed into your rate or passed through is a contract choice. We show you which on every offer.

  • Delivery: stays with your utility

    Wires, meters and outage response don't change when you switch.

Contract structure

The rate is half the job. The contract is the other half.

Competition gets you a good number. How the contract is built decides what you actually pay over its life — so we structure it around your load, not around whatever the supplier sent first.

Your contract, decided line by lineIllustration

Product

Fixed, index, or block-and-index. The right answer turns on whether your business can absorb a monthly swing — and we say plainly which one your load profile argues for.

Term length

Most commercial contracts run 12–36 months. A longer term is not automatically cheaper — it depends on where the forward curve sits when you sign. We advise on term against current market conditions.

Timing

We pull current forward curve data to time the purchase, and we shop inside your renewal window — not after it closes, when a lapsed contract rolls onto default or holdover pricing.

The fine print

Early termination language, usage bandwidth limits, and which charges are passed through rather than fixed. We normalize every offer so you are comparing like with like.

Across your sites

Operate several locations? Where it helps, we line up contract end dates so your portfolio can be shopped as one account instead of one meter at a time.

FAQ

Questions owners ask us first.

How do you get paid?

The supplier you choose pays us a per-kWh fee out of their margin — disclosed in your contract. You pay nothing out of pocket, and our recommendation is the same regardless of which supplier you pick. We're independent.

Are you a supplier or a broker?

A broker. We don't generate or sell electricity ourselves. We shop your account across 90+ retail energy suppliers in the BOX network — including Constellation, NRG/Direct Energy, AEP Energy, Engie, Shell Energy, and TXU — and bring you their best competing offers.

Will my service be interrupted when I switch?

No. Your local utility still delivers the power and reads your meter. We're swapping the supplier behind the meter, which happens at your next billing cycle with zero on-site change.

What if I'm mid-contract?

Send the bill anyway. We note your contract end date and shop inside your renewal window, so the new contract starts when the current one ends and you never break the one you're in. Some suppliers will also offer to "blend and extend" a current contract; we'll tell you plainly whether that's worth taking.

Which states do you cover?

Every US deregulated market — including TX, PA, NJ, NY, OH, IL, MD, MA, CT, DC, DE, ME, NH, RI, VA, and CA — plus deregulated gas markets across the country. If your business has sites in multiple states, we consolidate them under one agreement.

How long does the process take?

You spend about 5 minutes submitting your info. First competitive bids arrive after suppliers return pricing. When you pick a rate, you'll sign a Letter of Authorization and a one-page pricing contract — both take under 10 minutes. The switch happens on your next billing cycle, typically 2–4 weeks later.
