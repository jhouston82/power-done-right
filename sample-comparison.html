Part 4

What we check on every offer

  • The price type and contract length, against where the market sits today
  • That the start date lines up with your current contract's end — no gap, no overlap
  • Which grid charges are included in the price and which are passed along to you
  • Usage limits, and the penalty if your usage moves outside them
  • Early termination terms
  • Expected cost over the full contract, not just the headline rate
